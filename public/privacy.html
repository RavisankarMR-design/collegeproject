Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-20

What this app collects

  • Google account email and name — used to sign you in and identify your role (student/staff).
  • Roll number — self-entered, used to match you to a class roster.
  • GPS location — captured only at the moment you mark attendance, used to confirm you're inside the classroom radius. Not tracked at any other time.
  • A device identifier — derived from browser/hardware signals, used to stop one device marking attendance for multiple accounts.

How it's used

Solely to record and verify class attendance for this course. Data is visible to the teacher who runs the session and to the college. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Data retention

Attendance and session records are kept for the duration of the course for record-keeping purposes. Contact the app administrator to request deletion of your data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact the college staff member administering this app.
